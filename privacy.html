Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Crossing Times is a community-reported border wait-time app. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used, in plain language.

What we collect

When you submit a wait-time report, we store:

  • The border crossing you selected
  • The wait time and vehicle type you entered
  • An optional short note, if you added one
  • The time of your report

We do not collect your name, email, or any account information \u2014 the app doesn't require sign-in.

Location

If you tap "Use my location," your device's GPS coordinates are used only on your own device to sort crossings by distance and show a marker on the map. Your location is never sent to or stored on our servers.

How report data is used

Reports are shared with everyone using the app, in real time \u2014 that's the whole point: your report helps other travelers, and theirs help you. This data is public by design within the app.

Where data is stored

Reports are stored using Google Firebase (Firestore), Google's cloud database service. Data is retained only for a short rolling window (currently a few hours) since old wait-time reports aren't useful for current conditions.

Local storage on your device

The app saves a few small preferences directly on your device (not on our servers): your language choice, your theme preference, your favorited crossings, and a timestamp used to prevent rapid repeat submissions. You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app.

Third parties

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. No advertising or analytics trackers are used at this time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app developer through the contact details listed on the app's store page.
